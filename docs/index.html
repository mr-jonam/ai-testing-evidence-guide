The decision in focus

What evidence and decision ownership are needed to discuss acceptable AI behaviour?

Product owners and QA leaders evaluating an AI feature in an enterprise platform or digital product.

An orientation resource for a focused first conversation. Results reflect your answers and assumptions, not observed system behaviour.

What you can take away

  • Separate desired AI behaviour from a claim that a system is safe.
  • Frame evaluation, oversight and change questions using public risk references.
  • Prepare an AI testing discovery brief with no prompts or datasets attached.

A useful next step

Start with a focused business question.

Use the guide to name the business decision, try the mini tool to organise the open questions, then prepare a short collaboration request. A focused discovery conversation can clarify scope, evidence needs and whether further work would be useful.

Before you use this guide

Is this an AI security assessment?

No. It records discussion questions and produces no empirical evaluation or certification.

Does the skill send prompts to a model?

The website does not call an AI service. A SKILL.md is optional guidance for a separately chosen assistant; review that assistant’s data policy before using it.
